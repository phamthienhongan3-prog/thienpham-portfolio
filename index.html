Healthcare communications 02 / 04

Merck

The Year of Connections

I applied AI to produce a year-end communications video for Merck’s DEC diabetes education programme, bringing the supplied script to life through engaging visuals. The script was provided; my role was AI-assisted video production.

I applied AI in video production, working from the supplied script to bring physician meetings, patient stories and online learning together under the theme “The Year of Connections.” The creative brief emphasizes music and sound design, with Merck’s rich purple and vibrant magenta as the core colours.

Regional map scope: Oman, UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar and Yemen.

The script highlights more than 350 physicians at Diwani live meetings in Yemen and more than 2,800 online-course attendees. These figures describe the programme’s activities.
